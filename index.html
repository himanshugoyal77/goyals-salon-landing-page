Traditional foil highlights work from the scalp in symmetrical striped rows, often leaving an obvious harsh line of demarcation as your hair grows out. French Balayage is an artisanal, freehand sweeping method where lightness is customized to your haircut and natural movement. It grows out with zero harsh demarcation line, allowing 4 to 6 months between salon visits.
