# GOYALS SALON • Bespoke Hair Studio & Care Lab
### Modern, Responsive Luxury Landing Page

A modern, high-fashion, responsive one-page landing page inspired by the attached Behance editorial design concept board for **GOYALS SALON & Care Lab**.

---

## 🎨 Visual Style & Design System

- **Brand Name**: **GOYALS SALON** (Goyals Hair Studio & Care Lab)
- **Color Palette**:
  - `Cream Background`: `#FAF7F2`
  - `Linen Accent`: `#F5EFEB`
  - `Sand & Borders`: `#EAE3D9` / `#E2D8CC`
  - `Terracotta Wine (Signature Accent)`: `#9B3E32`
  - `Amber Gold Glow`: `#C59B6D`
  - `Deep Espresso / Ebony (Text & Dark Panels)`: `#181514` / `#221D1C`
  - `Muted Earth Taupe`: `#6B635E`

- **Typography**:
  - **Display Serif**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Google Fonts) – elegant high-contrast fashion editorial serif
  - **Clean Sans**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Google Fonts) – geometric, modern readability
  - **Signature Accent Script**: `Alex Brush` (Google Fonts) – delicate handwritten touch
  - **Editorial Accent**: `Playfair Display` (Google Fonts)

## 🚀 Sections & Features

1. **Top Announcement Ticker**: Studio alerts (*Flagships in SoHo NY & Le Marais Paris, Welcome to Goyals Salon*).
2. **Glassmorphism Navbar**: Sticky header with blur, `GOYALS.` logo, navigation links, shopping bag trigger with live counter badge, and "Book Appointment" button linking directly to `#booking`.
3. **Hero Section**:
   - High-impact headline: *"A place that cares about your hair."*
   - Subheadline & primary/secondary action buttons with smooth scrolling.
   - Key visual hero model in an arched editorial container with ambient backlight.
   - Floating glassmorphism cards: `★ 4.9/5 Rating` & `100% Bio-Active Care`.
   - Large background watermark typography: `GOYALS`.
4. **Brand Ethos / Philosophy (About Goyals)**:
   - Giant watermark: `ABOUT GOYALS`.
   - Editorial manifesto, 3 value pillars, salon interior collage, and achievement counter.
5. **Hair Shade Studio & Color Lab**:
   - Interactive shade filter tabs (*All, Blonde, Brunette, Copper, Noir*).
   - 4-column responsive grid with undertone badges and service recommendations.
6. **Signature Services & Treatment Menu**:
   - Filter pills (*All Rituals, Color & Balayage, Keratin Repair, Sculpture Cuts, Scalp Spa*).
   - 6 responsive service cards with pricing and duration.
7. **Curated Care Haircare Line**:
   - 4 luxury product cards with live slide-out shopping bag drawer and subtotal calculation.
8. **Editorial Lookbook & Transformations**:
   - 4-column visual gallery with hover reveal overlays and color formula notes.
9. **Social Proof & Client Testimonials**:
   - Interactive testimonials carousel slider with client portraits, quotes, and press endorsement banner.
10. **Interactive Goyals Salon Mobile App Mockup**:
    - Ultra-realistic smartphone device displaying the **Goyals Salon App**.
    - Live appointment reservation card (*Thu, Nov 12 • 10:30 AM*).
    - Scalp diagnostic score (*94% Cuticle Density*).
    - Interactive service chips & time slot selectors (*10:30 AM, 1:00 PM, 3:30 PM, 5:30 PM*).
    - Instant in-app booking confirmation with animated confirmation banner.
    - Preserves editorial text, studio locations, and concierge on the left side.
11. **FAQ Accordion**:
    - Expandable clean questions answering balayage, keratin formula, and scalp mapping.
12. **Luxury Editorial Footer**:
    - Newsletter subscription (`GOYALS15`), flagship addresses, and copyright.
